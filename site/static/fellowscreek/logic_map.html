— classification complete — player now decides —
Player decision
Choose: leave in place / cut & send downstream / remove entirely
Leave in place
Free — habitat benefit if size/orientation qualify. May trigger jam check if reach is accumulating.
Cut & send downstream
Low cost — debris risk transfers to others.
Remove entirely
High cost — clears reach but raises flood risk track.
— cascades to shared systems —
Jam structure check
Does the accumulated wood span the channel and form a step or backwater? If blockage ratio is high → flood risk track also rises. If not → habitat benefit only.

Flood risk track +1
Raised by removal — or by a jam crossing the blockage-ratio threshold. Amplifies storm events.

Core design tension
Cheap short-term fix (cut/remove) harms others now or later. Best outcome (leave) requires trust and correct classification.
